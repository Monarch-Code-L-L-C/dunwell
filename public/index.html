    What the law already gives you

    You do not need a late-fee clause in your contract. In the UK and across the EU, a business that is paid late by another business can claim interest and a fixed sum automatically.

    United Kingdom

    8% + base rate a year

    8% above the Bank of England base rate (see calculator for the current half-year), under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998.

    Plus fixed compensation: £40 on debts under £1,000, £70 up to £9,999.99, £100 from £10,000.

    European Union

    8 points + ECB rate a year, minimum

    At least 8 points above the ECB reference rate (see calculator for the current half-year), under Directive 2011/7/EU as implemented in each member state.

    Plus at least €40 compensation for recovery costs on every late invoice.

    Questions

    Can I charge interest if my contract says nothing about late payment?

    For business-to-business invoices in the UK and the EU, yes. The right is statutory and applies where the contract is silent. If your contract sets its own late-payment rate, that rate normally applies instead, so choose "Contract rate".

    Does this work for invoices to consumers?

    No. Statutory late-payment interest covers commercial debts between businesses, including sole traders and public bodies. For consumer debts use "Contract rate" or "No interest".

    Will claiming interest sour the relationship?

    The sequence starts friendly and mentions interest only at the third step, with an offer to waive it if the invoice is paid within a week. Most clients pay well before that. You decide which steps to send.

    I'm in the US, Canada, or elsewhere. Can I use it?

    Yes. Choose "Contract rate" and enter the rate from your contract or terms, per month or per year. Rules on maximum rates vary by state and country, so check yours.

    Where does my invoice data go?

    It is sent to our server to produce your figures and emails, and it is not saved there. Your tracker lives in your own browser. If you use the optional AI tailoring, the text of that one email is sent to our AI provider to be rewritten.

    Is this legal advice?

    No. Dunwell gives you accurate arithmetic and well-drafted correspondence based on published rates and legislation. For disputed debts, large sums, or before starting court proceedings, speak to a solicitor or lawyer.
